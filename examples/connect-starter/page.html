Connect Starter

Build an app from pieces. Each piece is one call on your app's account; the code under it is that call, in TypeScript or Python, ready to paste into your own app. Press the button and this page makes the same call, through this app's server (the only thing that holds the account's secret). What counts is the fact the account established itself (a transfer it accepted, a proof it verified, a signature it checked), never what a wallet says.
